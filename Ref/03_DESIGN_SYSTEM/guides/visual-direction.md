# Visual Direction

## North-star statement

**Enterprise in work. Premium in feel. Distinctive in moments that deserve attention.**

The base UI should feel calm, precise, compact, and mature. ZainX should not look like a generic shadcn admin template, but it also must not become a glowing concept UI.

## Visual layers

### Layer A — Quiet Foundation

Used for ~85–90% of the interface.

- Neutral surfaces.
- Thin borders.
- Strong text contrast.
- Restrained shadows.
- Compact spacing.
- Minimal gradients.
- Tables and forms dominate operational screens.

### Layer B — Operational Emphasis

Used for blockers, active workflow state, approval requests, exceptions, and meaningful warnings.

- Soft status tint.
- Stronger border or leading edge.
- Clear icon + label.
- Optional small illumination at the point of action.

### Layer C — Signature Spotlight

Used sparingly for:

- Main payroll readiness/outcome card.
- Major AI insight.
- Critical operational summary.
- Brand/login moments.
- Successful completion of high-risk workflows.

Allowed treatments:

- Soft radial spotlight.
- Border illumination on one edge or around pointer proximity.
- 5–10% tinted surface.
- Short-lived light sweep.
- Controlled gradient only inside the emphasized region.

Forbidden as normal UI:

- Full-screen neon ambience.
- Every card glowing.
- Constant pulsing borders.
- 3D tilt on finance/HR operational cards.
- Glass panels that reduce table readability.

## Card hierarchy

1. **Utility Card** — neutral, compact, no glow.
2. **Emphasis Card** — semantic tint or accent edge.
3. **Spotlight Card** — rare, visual focal point, can use controlled glow.

Recommended budget: generally one dominant Spotlight Card per major viewport region, with no more than two strong glow treatments visible above the fold.

## AI visual identity

Avoid the default "purple orb + sparkle" cliché as the sole identity. AI should be recognizable through:

- Context chips.
- Source/provenance rows.
- Animated context scan during processing.
- Relationship lines or source-to-answer movement.
- A subtle violet/cyan signal palette only for AI-specific states.

## Payroll visual identity

- Numeric hierarchy stronger than decorative graphics.
- Right/locale-aware alignment for money.
- Variance expressed with amount + percent + reason.
- Finalized/locked state visibly distinct from editable state.
- Readiness and blockers can receive stronger lighting than ordinary metrics.

## Brand-color note

The current accent palette is a working system palette. Final primary/brand tokens should be remapped once the real ZainX logo/vector brand assets are supplied.
