ZainX Workforce

SMARTER BUSINESS. ONE SYSTEM.

✦ Typeface: Sora #0A0F23 · #0D1B3D · #2563EB · #00E0C6

"The system is quiet. Important things glow." — A unified, production-grade enterprise design system for Workforce, HR, Payroll, Compliance, Recruitment, and AI. Supports ~65 screens, 8 canonical page patterns, Dark/Light modes, bilingual RTL/LTR, and 3 density modes.

Design Principles

Operational First

Users enter to approve, calculate, resolve, and act. Dashboards support work but never dominate workflows.

Quiet Foundation

85–90% of the interface is calm, neutral, and dense. Strong visual energy is reserved for what truly matters.

Light is an Event

Spotlight, glow, and illumination only for: major KPIs, AI insights, payroll outcomes, security, and brand moments.

Financial Trust

Payroll demands accounting-grade clarity. Numeric hierarchy, variance context, and audit traceability come first.

Permission-Aware

Every surface respects role, scope, and sensitivity. Access control is dignified, not playful.

Bilingual Native

Arabic RTL and English LTR from the same component system. Mixed-language content works naturally.
